## 摘要
在上一篇论文(arxiv.org/abs/1209.4772)中，我们基于贝叶斯估计提出了一种具有图像恢复的扩频水印模型，该模型假设了若干先验概率，并采用高斯信道模型表示来自未授权用户的攻击。当图像由无限范围Ising模型生成时，我们利用统计力学方法中的副本方法对该模型进行了分析，推导了副本对称(RS)解，并进行了马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗模拟。除了某些参数范围外，RS解的理论结果与模拟结果吻合良好。我们同时处理了已知原始图像的知情情况和原始消息与原始图像均未知的盲情况，发现只要嵌入率和攻击率较小，这两种情况之间的差异很小。在本文中，我们处理盲情况，研究RS解的de Almeida-Thouless(AT)稳定性，并揭示理论结果与模拟结果不一致的参数范围内AT稳定性被破坏。

## Abstract
In the previous paper (arxiv.org/abs/1209.4772), we proposed a spread-spectrum watermarking model with image restoration based on Bayes estimation assuming several prior probabilities and adopting the Gaussian channel model to represent attacks from unauthorized users. When an image is generated from the infinite range Ising model, we analyzed the model using the statistical mechanical method, the replica method, and derived the replica symmetric (RS) solution and performed Markov chain Monte Carlo simulations. The theoretical results of the RS solution and the simulation results were in good agreement except for some range of parameters. We treated the informed case where only the original image is known and the blind case where both the original message and the original image are unknown and found that the difference between these cases was small as long as the embedding and attack rates were small. In this paper, we treat the blind case and investigate the de Almeida-Thouless (AT) stability of the RS solution and reveal that the AT stability is broken in the range of parameters where theoretical and simulation results do not agree.
